title=Quick Restart [B42.20 Stable]
description=[img]https://i.imgur.com/Pu2nMwd.gif[/img]
description=
description=Adds a Quick Restart panel on the death screen that gets you back into the game significantly faster than the normal process.
description=Two options: Restart in a fresh world or respawn in the same one, both restoring your original character.
description=
description=Character is saved upon CREATION and reapplied automatically:
description=- Name, gender, profession
description=- Traits, skills and known recipes
description=- Full appearance: hair, beard, hair color, skin tone, body hair, hair stubble, beard stubble
description=- All worn clothing with colors and texture variants
description=- Voice type, pitch, and prefix
description=- Character weight
description=- Spawn region
description=
description=The sandbox settings are captured automatically shortly after spawning. If they have changed since your character was created, the Fresh World button will prompt you to choose between the original settings and the current ones.
description=
description=If you choose to "Continue with a new character" instead of using Quick Restart, that new character will become the new original that gets reloaded on the next Quick Restart.
description=
description=If this mod is enabled mid-save, the Quick Restart buttons will be disabled since no character data was saved, as it is saved on character creation. "Continue with a new character" once and Quick Restart will be available from your next death on.
description=
description=[img]https://i.imgur.com/gqWYFPL.gif[/img]
description=
description=A dice button on the Quick Restart panel opens the Randomizer Options window. Each category can be set to Keep (reapply your saved character) or Random:
description=- Gender: rolls a gender; anything transferable from your appearance is kept
description=- Appearance: rolls hair, beard, hair color and skin tone
description=- Name: rolls a new first and last name
description=- Occupation: rolls an occupation; the traits roll with it, since a new occupation changes the point budget
description=- Traits: rolls a fresh set of traits that respects the world's trait points and mutual exclusions
description=- Clothing: dresses you in a random outfit from the character creation outfit pool
description=- Spawn region: rolls a region among those available for the save, modded maps included
description=
description=Fresh World only:
description=- Seed: generates a new world seed
description=- Zombies: fully randomizes the zombie settings
description=- Sandbox settings: fully randomizes everything else, zombies follow the Zombies option. It is REALLY random and the world can turn out extreme or barely playable. You have been warned... twice, there is a warning popup too.
description=- Mod sandbox settings: fully randomizes the sandbox options added by your other mods (only shown when at least one active mod adds sandbox options)
description=
description=[img]https://i.imgur.com/Y6jzQpX.png[/img]
description=
description=Quick Restart touches a lot of game mechanics. It reads and rewrites:
description=- Character creation: name, gender, occupation and traits
description=- Skills, XP and known recipes
description=- Appearance and voice
description=- Worn clothing and starter kit items
description=- Character and descriptor mod data (this is how modded character customization usually survives a restart)
description=- Spawn regions and map selection
description=- World creation: sandbox settings and world seed
description=- The default mod list (Fresh World only, backed up and restored automatically)
description=- The death screen UI
description=
description=A mod that changes one of these mechanics in a way Quick Restart does not expect can cause compatibility issues. The most frequent one: the character or world data cannot be saved, and once you die the Quick Restart buttons tell you that no data was saved. If that happens to you, please post a message in the Bugs discussion with your mod list, I will see what I can do to widen the scenarios handled by the mod.
description=
description=Modded items that cannot be captured are simply skipped: they will not be reapplied on Quick Restart, everything else should be.
description=
description=[img]https://i.imgur.com/HQ2KJLs.png[/img]
description=
description=The game does not release all of its memory when a world is reloaded. This is an engine issue: it happens with or without this mod, but frequent restarts make it show up faster.
description=
description=Quick Restart keeps an eye on it for you:
description=- It estimates how many restarts remain before the game runs out of memory.
description=- When that gets low, a warning shows at the moment you use Quick Restart, never during gameplay.
description=- You can then close the game (the fresh world will load on its own as soon as you relaunch it) or continue without closing.
description=- The estimate calibrates itself to your machine and mod list over time.
description=
description=If you relaunch the game later instead of right away, a prompt at the main menu asks whether you still want that pending fresh world.
description=
description=Tip: The more zombies you have, the quicker you'll have to restart your game, so keep that in mind while setting up your next run!
description=
description=Hopefully The Indie Stone will fix this quickly and I'll be able to remove this, but in the meantime, it's the best I could do to mitigate it! Please let me know how it feels.
description=
description=[img]https://i.imgur.com/f9qefDM.png[/img]
description=
description=Q: Did you remove the normal death options?
description=A: No, they are all still there. Quick Restart even removes the vanilla delay, so they appear right away instead of slowly fading in.
description=
description=Q: Does it work in multiplayer?
description=A: Yes, same world Quick Restart works in multiplayer. Fresh world Quick Restart is single-player only and also disabled in split-screen. (You don't want to delete the world on which everyone else is playing!... Right?)
description=
description=Q: Does this give you back the XP you had before dying?
description=A: No. Your character is saved at creation, so you restart with your original starting skills and XP. Everything earned during the run is lost. It is a quick restart, not a resurrection.
description=
description=Q: I have a bug, what should I do?
description=A: Post a message in the Bugs discussion with your mod list and a description of what happened. If you can, paste the error from your console.txt (found in C:\Users\YourName\Zomboid), it makes finding the cause much faster.
description=
description=Q: I would like to suggest an improvement / I have an idea for a new feature, what should I do?
description=A: Post it in the comment section, I read everything. No promises, but good ideas have a way of ending up in the mod.
description=
description=Q: Can I randomize only some things, like appearance, and keep my build?
description=A: Yes. The dice button lets you set each category (gender, appearance, name, occupation, traits, clothing, spawn region, seed, zombies, sandbox, mod sandbox) to Keep or Random independently.
description=
description=Q: Can I restart from the main menu / without dying?
description=A: Not manually, the panel only lives on the death screen. The main menu will only resume a fresh world that was still pending when the game closed. If you just got bitten, enjoy your last moments.
description=
description=Q: I want to keep my skill levels and XP after dying, can this do that?
description=A: No, that is the opposite of what this mod is for. But I am working on a variant called Quick Respawn that will do exactly that, stay tuned.
description=
description=Q: Why are the Quick Restart buttons greyed out?
description=A: Either the mod was enabled mid-save, so no character data was captured (it happens at character creation), or you died too fast for the capture to complete (it runs moments after spawning). "Continue with a new character" once and the buttons will work from your next death on. If this keeps happening, there may be an important incompatibility with another mod, please let me know!
description=
description=Q: Does Fresh World delete my current world?
description=A: No. Fresh World generates a brand-new world as a separate save. The previous world is still there in the Load menu. Same World keeps playing in the same save.
description=
description=The mod is available in 29 languages.
description=
description=Feel free to provide any feedback you may have in the comment section.
